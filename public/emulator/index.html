What this is

Screen320 x 156 fitted canvas, the same geometry the device draws into.
Keypad6 by 9 matrix. Keyboard works on desktop: digits, operators, Enter, Backspace, Escape.
MathHandled by a local evaluator. The device routes this to the CAS.
AI armRuns on a recorded fixture. No key, no network, no cost.
Not builtSHIFT, ALPHA, MODE, ON, nCr, x!, M+ and S-D are placeholders in the stand-in.

The keys that are not wired print which one they are rather than pretending to work. The WebAssembly build replaces the evaluator; the screen and the keypad contract stay as they are.
